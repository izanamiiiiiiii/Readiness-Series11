This is a modified build, not an official WatchBridge/iloader release or endorsement.
It enables HealthKit for host/Watch App IDs declaring read access and verifies the resulting profiles before signing.
Sign in with your own Apple account. No account credentials, signing keys, or health data are bundled with this build.
Health access still requires your consent on the iPhone and Apple Watch.
The executable is not Authenticode-signed. Review BUILD.json, SHA256SUMS.txt, LICENSES.zip, and the included patch.
Physical Series 9/10 compatibility remains experimental.
